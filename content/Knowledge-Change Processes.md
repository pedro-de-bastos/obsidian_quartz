Coined by Chi and Wylie (2014, p. 223), 'knowledge-change' processes describe the four different procedures that may occur when one acquires new knowledge. These four procedures correspond to the four modes of engagement described in the [[ICAP Framework]]. They are:

- Store: new information is stored in an isolated way (passive). 
- Integrate: new information activates relevant prior knowledge and while storing, new information is integrated with activated prior knowledge (active). 
- Infer: new information is integrated with activated prior knowledge, and new knowledge is inferred from activated and integrated knowledge (constructive). 
- Co-Infer: Each learner infers new knowledge from activated and integrated knowledge and iteratively infers knowledge with new inputs from conversational partner(s) (interactive).
- (Chi and Wylie, 2014, p. 225)
