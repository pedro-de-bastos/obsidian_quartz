## John Dewey

1. **[[Democratic Education]]**:

Dewey argued that a democratic society requires citizens who are educated to think critically and participate actively. Schools should model democratic principles, teaching students about their rights and responsibilities as members of a democratic community.

2. **[[Experiential Learning]]**:

Dewey’s philosophy of education emphasizes learning through experience. He argued that traditional education, which focused on rote memorization and passive learning, was inadequate. Instead, he advocated for an interactive and hands-on approach to education, where students engage in activities that are meaningful and relevant to their lives.
