
Seeber et al. (2020) discuss how certain factors may be either improved or worsened, given the inclusion of an AI teammate in a team. They call this potential for improvement or worsening a 'concept dichotomy'. These are the main dichotomies they discuss:

**A. Concept Dichotomies** These describe a paradoxical effect within a single theoretical concept.

- **Affect:** Positive emotions (bonding) vs. Negative emotions (frustration/anger).
    
- **Knowledge:** Augmented team intelligence vs. Depleted human skills (deskilling/dependency).
    
- **Trust:** Building trust in recommendations vs. Losing trust (due to errors or contradictions).
    
- **Group Dynamics:** Positive cohesion vs. Negative conflict.
    
- **Health:** Safety enabler vs. Psychological/Physical risk.
    
- **Jobs:** Creation of creative jobs vs. Elimination of low-skill jobs.
    
- **Technology Acceptance:** Accepted vs. Rejected (technophobia).


Seeber, Isabella, Eva Bittner, Robert O. Briggs, et al. “Machines as Teammates: A Research Agenda on AI in Team Collaboration.” _Information & Management_ 57, no. 2 (2020): 103174. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.im.2019.103174](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.im.2019.103174).
