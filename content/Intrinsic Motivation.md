"the prototypic manifestation of the human tendency toward learning and creativity" - (Ryan and Deci)

"Perhaps more important, in the real world, motivation is highly valued because of its consequences: Motivation produces" - (Ryan and Deci)

People who are motivated intrinsically, rather than extrinsically, typically (Ryan and Deci):
- Have enhanced performance, persistence and creativity
- Show heightened vitality
- Higher self-esteem
- General well-being

Ryan, Richard M, and Edward L Deci. _Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being_. n.d.
