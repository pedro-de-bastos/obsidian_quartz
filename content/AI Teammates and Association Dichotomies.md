Seeber et al. (2020), in addition to discussing [[AI Teammates and Concept Dichotomies]], where they same concept/variable can be affected differently by an AI teammate depending on other conditions, also discuss how certain variables can impact others. They call this an 'association dichotomy'. These are the association dichotomies they highlight:

**B. Association Dichotomies** These describe a paradox where improving one concept negatively impacts an associated concept.

- **Decision Quality vs. Critical Thinking:** Higher quality decisions may lead to a reduced human capability to criticize or analyze information.
    
- **Pace vs. Cognitive Load:** Increased work pace may lead to increased cognitive overload for humans unable to keep up.
    
- **Creativity vs. Serendipity:** AI might increase creativity through data, but algorithms reinforcing existing views may reduce "out-of-the-box" serendipity.
    
- **Efficiency vs. Human Agency:** More efficient reasoning by AI may result in fewer human-driven decisions (loss of agency).
    
- **Acknowledgement vs. Expectations:** If AI contributions are acknowledged, employers may raise expectations for human output to unsustainable levels.
    
- **Anthropomorphism vs. Manipulation:** Making AI more human-like increases likeability but enables manipulation (by the AI or bad actors).
    
- **Responsibility vs. Control:** Assigning AI more responsibility (due to competence) leads to a loss of human control.
    
- **Visibility vs. Privacy:** To be effective, MaTs need data, leading to a loss of privacy.
    
- **Adaptiveness vs. Misbehavior:** Highly adaptive AI might learn bad behaviors (aggression, bias) from humans.
    
- **Value Creation vs. Power Shifts:** AI creates value but may cause extreme power shifts between those who own the machines and those who do not.

Seeber, Isabella, Eva Bittner, Robert O. Briggs, et al. “Machines as Teammates: A Research Agenda on AI in Team Collaboration.” _Information & Management_ 57, no. 2 (2020): 103174. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.im.2019.103174](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.im.2019.103174).
